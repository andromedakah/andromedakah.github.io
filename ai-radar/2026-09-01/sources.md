## The story — after taking the census of its agents, the enterprise meets the authority that asks it to produce the roll and prove it: EU AI Act enforcement became concrete this week with the first formal Requests for Information to frontier model providers

### The inspector — EU AI Act enforcement turns real (the datable hook)

- European Commission, "Commission starts enforcing AI Act rules and new transparency requirements on 2 August" — https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august (europa.eu unreachable to direct fetch; relayed via search summary)
- European Commission press release IP/26/1714 — https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1714 (unreachable to direct fetch; relayed via search summary)
- Wilson Sonsini, "EU AI Act: Enforcement Phase Begins" — https://www.wsgr.com/en/insights/eu-ai-act-enforcement-phase-begins.html
- Tokenstead, "EU AI Act: First Enforcement — Security RFIs" — https://tokenstead.ai/guides/eu-ai-act-first-enforcement-security-rfis (recipient list reported/unconfirmed)
- Help Net Security, "EU begins enforcing AI Act, putting AI models under the microscope" (4 August 2026) — https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/08/04/eu-ai-act-enforcement-ai-models/

Claims sourced here (as reported): the EU AI Act's general-purpose-AI (GPAI) obligations became enforceable on 2 August 2026, and new transparency requirements (Article 50 — disclosure of AI interaction, machine-readable labeling of AI-generated content and deepfakes) began to apply. On 29 August 2026 the Commission's AI Office reportedly sent its first formal Requests for Information (RFIs) to GPAI providers — reported to include OpenAI, Anthropic and Google — covering model security, independent external evaluations, and post-market model monitoring. The recipient list is reported/unconfirmed and is flagged as such. Fines run up to 3% of global revenue for GPAI obligations (higher tiers apply to prohibited uses). Article 12 requires automatic event logging built for reconstructability. Models placed on the market before 2 August 2025 have until 2 August 2027 to reach full compliance. Carried here as the "inspector": compliance stopped being a policy document and became a letter with a deadline, backed by enforcement.
