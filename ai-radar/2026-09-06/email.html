1 · Executive Summary

For two weeks this radar built, counted, inspected, secured, questioned, priced and reflexively improved the agentic workforce. This morning the frame meets its loudest event yet: not a new capability but a new claim about all of them. A frontier lab has proclaimed that the age itself has arrived — and the question is not whether that is thrilling (it is) but whether you should reprice your strategy on it.

1. The herald rang the bell. OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra on 3 September, and president Greg Brockman called it "the start of AGI," welcoming "the AGI era" (Fortune, Bloomberg, TechCrunch). It is built to use a computer (~72.6% on OSWorld 2.0, as reported), arrived "powerful and controversial," and shipped with added cyber guardrails, business users first via "Daybreak." A model that can operate a machine can do the work of a hundred hands — and open a hundred locks.

2. The ledger has not moved as fast as the bell. McKinsey State of AI 2026: ~40% of large orgs scaling agents (from ~27%), ~80% report productivity gains — but only ~37% any EBIT, ~93% over budget, ~60% of agentic cost on "response refinement." PwC (4,454 CEOs): only ~12% booked both cost and revenue benefits, ~56% no significant benefit (as reported; Jan data). Productivity is everywhere; earnings are not.

3. The warning is real regardless. A cyber-capable "AGI-era" model is exactly what the regulator's systemic-risk regime is for. EU AI Act GPAI enforcement is live since 2 August 2026 (model eval, red-teaming, incident reporting for models >10²⁵ FLOPs); GPAI fines to €15M or 3% of turnover (top tier €35M / 7%) (as reported). (Prior editions' "15 Sep deadline" is corrected: the milestone is 2 Aug enforcement.)

Bottom line: a herald in the square proclaims the age of gold has come. The proclamation may even be right — but right about the age is not the same as arrived in your ledger. Do the two unglamorous things at once: don't reprice your strategy on a herald's word — keep counting your own sacks, quarter by quarter, against EBIT — and take the warning dead seriously, minding every lock the new tool can open. A claim about the age is not flour in the storehouse, and a tool that can open any door has already changed what you must guard.

2 · Allegory — The Herald's Proclamation

A valley lived and died by its harvest, and measured its fortunes the only honest way there is — by walking into the storehouse at year's end and counting the sacks. Then one morning a herald climbed the tower and rang the great bell: "The age of gold has come! A new tool is here that can do the work of a hundred hands — read any ledger, open any door, labor without rest. Rejoice." Half the square ran to reprice everything and borrowed against it; half barred their doors and panicked. Both had let the bell set the value of their year, when the only thing that had happened was that a man had rung it.

A few households did neither. They walked into their own storehouse and counted — and the storehouse told a plainer tale. The tool was astonishing, every hand worked faster, and yet, weighed against the year before, the flour had barely risen. Everyone busier, almost no one richer; the gains dissolved into more grinding of the same grain. And they heard the other half of the proclamation the celebrants had missed — that the tool could open any door — and went and checked every lock it could pick, because a tool that can open any door is a blessing in the owner's hand and a curse in a thief's, and it does not ask which holds it. When the celebrating households came back from their repriced, over-borrowed year with empty sacks and an open door, the counters were the ones still standing.

The question it forces: A lab has proclaimed the age of AGI, loud and repriced into every valuation. So the question is not "is the tool remarkable" — it plainly is. It is whether we reprice our strategy on a herald's word or on our own ledger, which still says productivity is everywhere and EBIT is not; and whether we heard the other half — that the tool can open any door — and checked every lock it can pick, because the inspector is already in the square asking us to prove we did. When the bell rings for the age of gold, do we run to reprice — or count our sacks and mind our locks?

3 · Technical — Reading the Proclamation From the Storehouse

A proclamation moves three fast clocks and one slow one: it changes expectations, valuations and adversary capability instantly, and the flour in your storehouse slowly, if at all. The bell: OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra (3 Sep); Greg Brockman — "the start of AGI"; agentic and computer-using (~72.6% OSWorld 2.0, as reported), "powerful and controversial," added cyber guardrails. The significant fact is not the "AGI" label but that the model can act on systems — the most valuable and most double-edged thing to arrive; the guardrails are the tell. The ledger: McKinsey 2026 — ~40% of large orgs scaling agents (from ~27%), ~80% productivity vs only ~37% EBIT, ~93% over budget, ~60% of agentic cost on "response refinement"; PwC (4,454 CEOs) — ~12% both cost and revenue benefits, ~56% no benefit. The tool made every hand faster and the sacks barely heavier, because the process was not rebuilt. The locks: EU AI Act GPAI enforcement live since 2 Aug 2026; systemic-risk duties (eval, red-team, incident reporting) for models >10²⁵ FLOPs; fines to €15M / 3% (top tier €35M / 7%). Run strategy on the slow clock, defense on the fast one.

5 · So What — 3 Moves for the Next 30 Days

1. Run strategy on the ledger, not the bell. No plan, budget or board promise gets repriced on a claim about the age — only on our own storehouse, measured against EBIT, quarter by quarter. Report the share of AI spend tied to a measured EBIT or hard-cost outcome versus the share justified by capability, benchmark or proclamation — and separate productivity (~80%) from earnings (~37%).

2. Count the flour — measure yield, not activity. ~93% of AI teams are over budget and ~60% of agentic cost goes to "response refinement." Require a storehouse metric — output that reaches the P&L — on every initiative, and reallocate toward the process redesigns that move the harvest, not the extra agents.

3. Mind the locks — treat the capability as symmetric. Check every door an agentic, machine-operating model can now open in our estate; bound what it may touch; and be ready to show the model evaluation, red-teaming and incident-reporting the EU AI Office now requires. Report the share of agentic/computer-using deployments with mapped, bounded, monitored permissions.
