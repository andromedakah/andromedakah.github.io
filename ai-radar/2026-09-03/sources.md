## The story — after building the agentic workforce, counting it (the census), facing the inspector and pricing the break-in, the enterprise asks the treasurer's question: after all this motion, are we grinding any flour? This week the data answered — enterprise AI is a two-speed race, with record adoption and flat earnings for all but a rewiring few

Claims sourced here (as reported): McKinsey's *State of AI 2026* survey (1,719 respondents across 97 countries; fielded 4 May–8 June 2026; published 25 August 2026) reports that among organizations with more than $1 billion in annual revenue, 40% are now scaling AI agents, up from 27% a year earlier; that 32% of organizations have decided against buying off-the-shelf software, building their own with agentic coding tools instead (nearly half of high performers vs 31% of peers); that just ~6% of respondents qualify as "AI high performers" (attributing at least 5% of EBIT to AI and describing its impact as significant), a share unchanged from last year; that ~37% attribute at least some EBIT impact to AI, "about the same" as last year; and that high performers are nearly three times as likely as others to fundamentally redesign their workflows end to end. Re-circulated on 2 September (AIwire) under the frame "enterprise AI is becoming a two-speed race." Carried here as the "two mills": deploying agents (turning the wheel) is not the same as capturing value (grinding flour) — the differentiator is process redesign, not agent count.

### The cancellations — the cost of motion without milling (why it is a board problem)

- Gartner Newsroom, "Gartner Predicts Over 40% of Agentic AI Projects Will Be Canceled by End of 2027" — https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027 (via search summary)
- Forbes, "Why 40% Of Agentic AI Projects May Be Canceled By 2027" — https://www.forbes.com/sites/robertszczerba/2026/07/07/why-40-of-agentic-ai-projects-may-be-canceled-by-2027/ (via search summary)
- BigDATAwire, "Gartner Predicts Over 40% of Agentic AI Projects Will Be Canceled by End of 2027" — https://www.hpcwire.com/bigdatawire/this-just-in/gartner-predicts-over-40-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027/ (egress-blocked; via search summary)

Claims sourced here (as reported): Gartner predicts that more than 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027, due to escalating costs, unclear business value or inadequate risk controls. Gartner Senior Director Analyst Anushree Verma: "Most agentic AI projects right now are early stage experiments or proof of concepts that are mostly driven by hype and are often misapplied." Gartner also notes widespread "agent washing" — the rebranding of AI assistants, RPA and chatbots without substantial agentic capability — estimating that only about 130 of the thousands of agentic AI vendors are "real." Carried here as the "empty mills being shut": a pilot with no baseline, no redesigned process and no P&L owner is motion instrumented as motion, and a prime candidate for cancellation.
