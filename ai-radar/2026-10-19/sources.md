*Key figures (as reported across the syndicated coverage above): ~850–858 IT decision-makers;
fieldwork 16–29 June 2026 (conducted by CIO Dive); 82% confident they can keep pace governing,
integrating and monitoring AI agents; 26% well prepared to resolve conflicts across agents,
systems and automated workflows; 74% rank integration with existing applications first; 63%
agent orchestration across systems; 62% retain the option to switch models/vendors/platforms;
35% cite data-consistency and context-sharing as the hardest orchestration hurdle; 71% expect
apps to autonomously coordinate within five years; KPMG's Michael Harper on assurance "built in
from the start," with the remedy framed as a shared control plane, policy-as-code and runtime
guardrails.*
