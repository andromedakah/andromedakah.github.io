Claims sourced here (as reported): On 13 August 2026, IBM and OpenAI announced a strategic partnership to help enterprises deploy AI at scale across core business operations. The partnership targets financial services, government, telecommunications and retail, and enterprise domains including finance, procurement, customer operations and HR; cybersecurity collaboration runs through OpenAI's Daybreak Cyber Partner Program combined with IBM Autonomous Security. IBM CEO Arvind Krishna is reported to have said AI remains a long-term growth driver, complementing rather than replacing demand, and had earlier acknowledged IBM "did not move quickly enough" to shifting Q2 customer spending. Forrester characterized the deal as validating OpenAI's "push-pull-partner" enterprise strategy.
