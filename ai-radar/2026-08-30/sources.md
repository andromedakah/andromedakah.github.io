## The story — the model is rented (and now metered), the knowledge is the moat — and this weekend the market proved it from both ends of the stack: the engine is a utility (NVIDIA) and a frontier model performs inside the incumbent's app over your own data (Claudeforce)

### The engine — a metered, booming utility (the datable hook, bottom of the stack)

- NVIDIA Investor Relations / Newsroom, "NVIDIA Announces Financial Results for Second Quarter Fiscal 2027" (26 August 2026) — https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx
- Fortune, "NVIDIA results: Q2 earnings" (26 August 2026) — https://fortune.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-results-q2-earnings/
- CNBC, "NVIDIA (NVDA) earnings report Q2 FY2027 — live updates" (26 August 2026) — https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q2-2027-live-updates.html

Claims sourced here (as reported): NVIDIA reported second-quarter fiscal 2027 results on 26 August 2026 with total revenue of roughly US$96.2 billion (up ~106% year over year), data center revenue of roughly US$89.0 billion (up ~117% year over year, ~92% of the total), gross margin ~75%, and Q3 guidance of roughly US$108 billion (±2%). On the earnings call, CEO Jensen Huang forecast ~70% revenue growth for fiscal 2028 and framed the reasoning engine as a metered utility: "AI has reached its inflection point… Now, compute is revenue. And demand is accelerating." Carried here as the "engine": the reasoning tier has been commoditized into metered tokens, and the vendors supplying it are capturing the value.

### The repertoire — the frontier model performs inside the incumbent's app over your data (top of the stack)

- Salesforce Newsroom, "Salesforce and Anthropic Announce Claudeforce" (26 August 2026) — https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/08/26/salesforce-and-anthropic-announce-claudeforce/
- VentureBeat, "Salesforce just put its entire CRM inside Claude — and says you'll never need its app again" — https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/salesforce-just-put-its-entire-crm-inside-claude-and-says-youll-never-need-its-app-again
- CNBC, "Salesforce-Anthropic partnership: Claudeforce" (Benioff–Amodei interview, 26 August 2026) — https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/salesforce-anthropic-partnership-claudeforce.html

Claims sourced here (as reported): On 26 August 2026 Salesforce and Anthropic announced "Claudeforce," making Claude the default reasoning model across Agentforce and Slack, plus a "Salesforce in Claude" surface with 37 prebuilt sales skills (open beta slated for September 2026), deployed inside the Salesforce Trust Boundary via Amazon Bedrock for regulated industries. Marc Benioff: "By fusing Claude's extraordinary reasoning with the trusted data, workflows, and governance every enterprise runs on, we're delivering a dynamic interface that thinks, reasons, and acts. This is how every business will run." Dario Amodei (Salesforce newsroom): "Companies can point Claude at the customer information and business context that they've been building in Salesforce for decades, and use it to actually run and grow their businesses." Dario Amodei (CNBC): "This is the way all enterprise systems are going to run in the future." Carried here as the "repertoire": the reasoning engine is rented; the proprietary corpus, workflows, governance and governed boundary are the asset — and the frontier lab itself locates the value in the customer's own data.

Claims sourced here (as reported): OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar reportedly told investors (~14 August 2026) that enterprise revenue now exceeds consumer ChatGPT revenue, with an annualized run rate of ~US$40 billion (up from ~US$20 billion at the start of 2026). ### The standards — portability that keeps the engine swappable (context)

- Forbes (Janakiram MSV), "Agent2Agent Joins the Agentic AI Foundation Alongside MCP" (19 August 2026) — https://www.forbes.com/sites/janakirammsv/2026/08/19/agent2agent-joins-the-agentic-ai-foundation-alongside-mcp/
- Linux Foundation, "Linux Foundation Announces the Formation of the Agentic AI Foundation" — https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-the-formation-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation
- Model Context Protocol Blog, "The New MCP Roadmap" (22 August 2026) — https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/mcp-roadmap/

Claims sourced here (context, as reported): Google's Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol became a hosted project of the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) at the Linux Foundation (mid-August 2026), joining Anthropic's MCP, Block's goose and OpenAI's AGENTS.md under vendor-neutral governance; reporting cites AAIF membership of 250+ (AWS, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, Bloomberg, Cloudflare and others) and an MCP public registry of 10,000+ servers. Carried here as the layer that lets an enterprise rent one engine and move to the next — portability is what keeps the metered engine swappable.

- European Commission, "Commission starts enforcing AI Act rules and new transparency requirements on 2 August" — https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august

Claims sourced here (as reported): As of 2 August 2026 the European Commission's AI Office, with national authorities, began enforcing the AI Act, and Article 50 transparency rules began to apply; Article 12 requires automatic event logging built for reconstructability. Fines run up to the higher of €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover. Carried as context: a rented engine performing your proprietary repertoire must still perform it inside a governed, auditable boundary you control.
