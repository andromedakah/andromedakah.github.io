- Crypto Briefing, "AMD CEO Lisa Su says AI is at an inflection point for meaningful work" — https://cryptobriefing.com/amd-lisa-su-ai-inflection-point-meaningful-work/

Claims sourced here (as reported): At AMD's flagship 2026 event, CEO Lisa Su characterized 2026 as a historical milestone — the first year global inference compute is set to surpass training compute. She put monthly AI token processing at roughly 35 quadrillion tokens, about 160× the volume of two years earlier, with inference accounting for around 60% of capacity, attributing the shift to the rise of agentic AI. The "waterworks" allegory (a city that raised a great dam only to find its fortune now turns on the water metered through every tap) is the radar's framing.
