How this ledger is kept: each daily brief that cites a dated, falsifiable forecast — a named source, a specific number, a due date — adds one line here. Most days nothing else changes; before publishing, the radar glances at what is due this week and resolves anything whose date has arrived, checked against current trackers rather than assumed. Figures are relayed from the sources named in each brief's sources.md and are marked "as reported" where a primary page could not be fetched directly; a status stamp reflects the best current public reading, not a guarantee.
