How to read the stamps

Hit — due date reached and the forecast held Miss — due date reached and it did not Pending — logged, due date still ahead Ongoing — a recurring benchmark that is re-checked, not closed

Each claim is paraphrased from the edition that first cited it; the full source link lives in that edition's sources.md. Figures are relayed from the named source and, where they rest on secondary coverage, were marked "as reported" in the original brief. A stamp is resolved against a current source when its due date arrives — not assumed. This log is kept for its own sake: a forecast you cannot go back and grade is an opinion, not a prediction.
