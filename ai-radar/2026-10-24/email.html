The Deferred Assize

Saturday, 24 October 2026 · Cross-sector edition · C-level + Engineering

The EU's Digital Omnibus just pushed the AI Act's hardest deadline sixteen months — standalone high-risk to 2 December 2027. But a reckoning deferred is not cancelled: the duty to disclose AI and label what it makes did not move, your buyers never set a date you can defer, and the model shelf refilled twice in a fortnight. Treat the reprieve as runway, not relief.

1 · Executive Summary

The axis moves from building controls to the day the outside authority checks them — the assize — and what a house does when the magistrate moves that day back sixteen months.

1. The reality — the magistrate deferred the great assize. The EU's Digital Omnibus on AI defers the AI Act's standalone high-risk obligations (Annex III — hiring, credit scoring, education, law enforcement, border control) to 2 December 2027 (≈16 months), and product-embedded systems (Annex I — medical devices, machinery) to 2 August 2028 (reported as Reg (EU) 2026/1744; CSA; Gibson Dunn; Travers Smith; DLA Piper, as reported). A hard deadline became a soft one — and that relief is the danger.

2. The collision — the honest duties stayed, and so did the buyers. Article 50 transparency still binds from 2 August 2026 — disclose AI, label AI-made content — with only a watermarking grace to 2 December 2026 and no further relief from the AI Board's September plenary (CSA, 21 Sep 2026); advisers warn the extension is "not an invitation to pause"; prEN 18286 only reached public enquiry in late 2025; and the model shelf refilled (Meta's first frontier models Muse Wonder 12 Oct; Gemini 4 Argon; Haiku 5.5 7 Oct; Mistral Large 4 weights due this week) (CSA; TechCrunch; Axios, as reported). The clock slipped; the risk did not.

3. The answer — runway, not relief. Keep the governance crew building to 2 December 2027 as a milestone; ship the Article 50 disclosure/labelling duties and the buyers' ISO-42001 bar that fell due regardless; and spend the sixteen months closing the readiness gap so the day arrives and you are ready.

Bottom line: the livery, steward, surety, second wage, two harvests, purse and foundation each built a control — the assize is the day an outsider checks them. The magistrate moved that day sixteen months, and most of the valley heard only "relief." But a reckoning deferred is not cancelled: the day still comes, a crew stood down is slow to re-raise, and the plain duty of honesty (disclose AI, label what it makes) did not move at all. Treat the sixteen months as runway, not relief — the magistrate moved the day, not the danger.

2 · Allegory — The Deferred Assize

A great house had made itself good over hard seasons — servants named, booked, bonded, waged, sent to reap, funded, and standing on ground it owned. Over all of it hung a date: the magistrate's day of assize, when every house running dangerous engines (ones that judge a loan, sort applicants, weigh a patient, watch the border) must stand before the bench and produce its papers. The whole valley raced toward it. Then the crier came: the assize is put off — sixteen months. And a hundred tired masters said the same thing: "Thank God. Stand the crew down. We have years."

But the wise houses noticed three things. A day deferred is not a day cancelled — later always comes, and a crew dismissed on Tuesday is not whole and skilled on Wednesday; the house that treats sixteen months as relief arrives as unready as before, only older. The magistrate moved the assize, not the plain law of honesty — the older rule still bound now: tell a man when he speaks to a machine, and mark what a machine makes. And the magistrate was never the only one keeping time — the buyers at the gate had set no date an Omnibus could move, and the measuring-rods the bench would use were still being forged.

So the wise houses did three plain things: do not stand the crew down (re-mark the calendar, keep building to the new day as a milestone passed early); pay the honest debts that fell due regardless (disclose the machine, mark its work, satisfy the buyers who never moved their date); and spend the runway on the readiness gap (count the engines, assemble the papers, track the rods) — so that when the crier returns, two winters on, the answer is a shrug and an open ledger, not a scaffold thrown up overnight.

The question it forces: Our hardest deadline just moved sixteen months — did we win runway, or lose the forcing-function that was getting our governance built? Did we keep the crew on the wall, or stand it down the morning the crier came? And did we notice that the one duty never deferred — tell a person they deal with a machine, and mark what it makes — fell due this season, while we were busy being relieved?

3 · Technical — From the Foundation to the Assize

Every prior control is something you build; the assize is the day an outsider checks. A deadline is not a risk — and when the deadline moves without the risk moving, every house that built to the date rather than to the danger is suddenly building to nothing. The reality: what moved is the weight — the QMS, conformity assessment, Annex IV technical docs, registration; what did not move is Article 50 transparency (disclose AI, label AI content), binding from 2 Aug 2026 with a watermarking grace to 2 Dec 2026 — usually the smaller lift, which makes neglecting it the least defensible outcome. The collision: "ready" will eat the runway (prEN 18286 only reached enquiry late 2025; ISO 42001 alone doesn't grant presumption of conformity), and the buyers' bar never moved while the model shelf refilled (Meta's first frontier models, Gemini 4 Argon, Haiku 5.5, Mistral Large 4). The answer: build to the risk and the milestone, not the deadline — re-baseline, ship the honest duties now, close the readiness gap.

5 · So What — 3 Moves for the Next 30 Days

1. Do not stand the crew down — re-baseline, don't disband. The deferral to 2 December 2027 tempts you to cut the budget and reassign the team — the one move you'll regret. Re-baseline your governance plan to the new dates as milestones you hit early, keep the team and cadence intact, and write down the risk/customer drivers that justify the programme independent of any regulator's calendar. Report the share of milestones tied to risk/customer drivers versus a statutory date alone.

2. Pay the honest debts that did not move. Article 50 transparency binds now (disclose AI, label AI content from 2 Aug 2026; watermark existing systems by 2 Dec 2026), and your buyers' ISO-42001 / lineage bar never had a date to defer. Audit every customer- and citizen-facing AI for disclosure, label your AI-generated content, and close the nearest buyer gaps. Report the share of customer-facing AI that discloses it is AI today.

3. Spend the runway on the readiness gap. "Deferred" is not "cancelled," "ready" still gates on standards not yet finalised, and the model shelf keeps refilling — every new deployment a new system to govern. Complete a high-risk-system inventory (including the newest models you've adopted), build the conformity-assessment and technical-documentation evidence against ISO 42001 and the draft standard, and watch prEN 18286. Report the count inventoried versus estimated, and a dated plan to reach conformity evidence well before 2 December 2027.
