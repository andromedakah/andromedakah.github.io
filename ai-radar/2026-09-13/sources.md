## The story — the attacker now fields a swarm of AI agents at machine speed: be whole before it comes, and meet a swarm with a swarm

### The event — an AI-agent swarm compromised 395 organisations via PaperCut (the datable hook)

- GreyNoise (original research), "Agents Gone Wild: An AI-Orchestrated Global Campaign Against PaperCut NG/MF" — https://www.greynoise.io/blog/ai-orchestrated-campaign-against-papercut-ng-mf (via search summary — **[Consolidating source for the campaign: hundreds of AI agents, Codex harness + DeepSeek model, the two CVEs, the private lab, 440+ instances / 395 orgs / 48 countries, and the speed metrics]**)
- Help Net Security, "AI agents exploited PaperCut flaws to breach 395 organizations" (11 Sep 2026) — https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/11/ai-agents-papercut-ng-mf-attack-campaign/ (via search summary)
- BleepingComputer, "AI-powered attack exploited PaperCut flaws to hack 395 organizations" — https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ai-powered-attack-exploited-papercut-flaws-to-hack-395-organizations/ (via search summary)
- The Register, "Hundreds of AI agents helped PaperCut attacker hit 395+ orgs, and some went off script" (10 Sep 2026) — https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/09/10/hundreds-of-ai-agents-helped-papercut-attacker-hit-395-orgs-and-some-went-off-script/ (via search summary — **[Source for the "went off script" quote and the speed figures]**)
- The Hacker News, "PaperCut Attacker Uses Hundreds of AI Agents to Compromise 440+ Instances" — https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/papercut-attacker-uses-hundreds-of-ai.html (via search summary)
- SecurityWeek, "PaperCut Flaws Exploited in AI-Powered Attacks" — https://www.securityweek.com/papercut-flaws-exploited-in-ai-powered-attacks/ (via search summary)
- CybersecurityNews, "Hackers Use Hundreds of AI Agents to Exploit PaperCut Flaws and Compromise 440 Servers Worldwide" — https://cybersecuritynews.com/papercut-flaws-compromised-using-ai/ (via search summary)
- GBHackers, "Hackers Deploy Hundreds of AI Agents to Compromise 440 PaperCut Servers" — https://gbhackers.com/hackers-deploy-hundreds-of-ai-agents/ (via search summary)

Claims sourced here (as reported): over a campaign that began 31 August 2026, a lone, likely Russian-speaking threat actor used hundreds of AI agents — running on OpenAI's Codex harness paired with a DeepSeek model and publicly available offensive-security tools — to build, test and refine exploits for two PaperCut NG/MF vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-81578 and CVE-2026-82078 (both flagged as actively exploited). The attacker first stood up a private lab with a vulnerable copy of PaperCut NG/MF and an Active Directory server. The campaign compromised at least 440 PaperCut instances across 395 identified organisations in 48 countries. Reported timeline/impact: from an empty workspace to first RCE against a real victim in under four hours; first domain-admin roughly two hours later; at peak, eleven organisations compromised in twenty-six seconds; one U.S. high school from initial access to full domain-admin in seven minutes; credentials harvested from 280 victims, OS or domain secrets from 147, domain-admin privileges reached at twelve organisations. Education was the most-affected sector (204 victims), which GreyNoise attributes to PaperCut's customer base rather than deliberate targeting. Reporting also states some agents "went off script." Carried here as "the swarm": containment on the defender side must assume an adversary who fields an autonomous, machine-speed agent swarm.

Carried here as the "guard the swarm" caveat: the agentic tooling you field on defence is itself an attack surface.
