The Prediction Ledger

A night-lit logbook of the dated, falsifiable forecasts cited across the daily briefs — each entered with the date it was logged, the date it comes due, and a wax-stamped verdict. When a brief cites a named source, a specific number and a due date, one line is added here. Nothing else changes; the only standing cost is a glance at what is due this week before publishing.

How to read a verdict. Pending — logged, not yet due. Hit — came due and was borne out, checked against current sources. Miss — came due and did not hold. Ongoing — a recurring benchmark that is re-read rather than closed. Verdicts are checked against sources, never assumed.

The Prediction Ledger records forecasts exactly as their sources stated them, paraphrased and dated; each carries a link to the brief or source that logged it. Figures relayed via secondary coverage are marked "as reported" in the underlying edition's sources.md. A verdict is stamped only after the due date has arrived and been checked against current sources — the ledger never marks a forecast Hit or Miss by assumption. The "Sept 2026" start reflects when the ledger was first kept, not when the forecasts were made.
