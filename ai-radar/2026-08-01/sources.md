## The story — the enforcement deadline arrives tomorrow, on a schedule fixed since 2024

### EU AI Act Article 50 transparency + GPAI enforcement (binding 2 August 2026)

- European Commission, "Transparency obligations under Article 50 of the AI Act" (FAQ) — https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act

- artificialintelligenceact.eu, "The EU AI Act's Transparency Rules: A Practical Guide to Article 50" — https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/transparency-rules-article-50/
- Cloud Security Alliance, "EU AI Act Article 50: Transparency Obligations Take Effect" — https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-article-50-transparency-20260729/
- Stibbe, "The AI Act's Transparency Obligations: Rules, Scope and Timeline" — https://www.stibbe.com/publications-and-insights/the-ai-acts-transparency-obligations-rules-scope-and-timeline
- accuroai, "EU AI Act: What Actually Applies on August 2, 2026" — https://accuroai.co/blog/eu-ai-act-what-actually-applies-august-2-2026
- Gibson Dunn, "EU AI Act Omnibus Agreement — Postponed High-Risk Deadlines and Other Key Changes" — https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/

Claims sourced here: On 2 August 2026, Article 50 transparency obligations become enforceable across all 27 member states — disclosure that a person is interacting with an AI; machine-readable marking of AI-generated content; deepfake and public-interest-text labeling; emotion-recognition and biometric-categorization notice — regardless of whether the underlying system is "high-risk" under Annex III. National market-surveillance authorities can enforce from that date and the AI Office begins to fine, taking exclusive competence over AI systems built on general-purpose AI (GPAI) models. Non-compliance can attract fines of up to the higher of €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover (Article 99). Systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 benefit from a four-month grace period (until 2 December 2026) before the machine-readable watermarking obligation of Article 50(2) applies to them. The high-risk (Annex III) standalone-system obligations were deferred to 2 December 2027 under the Digital Omnibus package. The enforcement date has been fixed in the Act's timetable since the Regulation ((EU) 2024/1689) entered force in 2024.

## The readiness gap on the eve

- Vision Compliance, "2026 EU AI Act Readiness Report, Finds 78% of Enterprises Unprepared for Obligations" (via National Law Review) — https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/vision-compliance-releases-2026-eu-ai-act-readiness-report-finds-78
- EIN Presswire, "Vision Compliance Releases 2026 EU AI Act Readiness Report, Finds 78% of Enterprises Unprepared for Obligations" — https://www.einnews.com/pr_news/903074846/vision-compliance-releases-2026-eu-ai-act-readiness-report-finds-78-of-enterprises-unprepared-for-obligations
- Cloud Security Alliance, "EU AI Act High-Risk Deadline: Enterprise Readiness Gap" — https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-high-risk-compliance-deadline-20/

Claims sourced here (as reported): Vision Compliance's 2026 EU AI Act Readiness report found that 78% of organizations have not taken meaningful steps toward AI Act compliance; that 83% of organizations assessed had no formal inventory of the AI systems they use or deploy; and that 74% lacked a designated internal owner or governance body for AI compliance — across financial services, healthcare, technology, manufacturing, energy, retail, telecommunications and transport. Senior Consultant Robert Gelo is quoted: "Most organizations are aware the AI Act exists, but very few understand what it actually requires of them. The regulation goes well beyond policy statements." Readiness coverage separately reports that 88% of organizations actively use AI across business functions while only 8% run a comprehensive AI governance program.
