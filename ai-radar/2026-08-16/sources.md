Claims sourced here (as reported): On or about 15 August 2026, OpenAI began notifying Free-tier and $8/month Go-tier users in the EEA and Switzerland that ads would appear in ChatGPT "later this month," extending an ad program first launched in the United States in February 2026 and expanded to the United Kingdom, Mexico, Brazil, Japan and South Korea by 11 August 2026. Ads appear on the Free and Go tiers; Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu accounts remain ad-free. OpenAI states — via Fidji Simo, its CEO of Applications / Chief Product Officer — that "ads will not influence the answers ChatGPT gives you," that ads are clearly labeled and separated from the organic answer, that conversations are kept private from advertisers, and that user data is not sold; OpenAI's US privacy policy was rewritten in 2026 to permit receiving purchase data from advertisers and their partners. The Fidji Simo quotation is relayed from secondary coverage of OpenAI's advertising materials and should be re-verified at source before republishing.

- AIwire / HPCwire, "AI Trust and Security Consortium launches to set peer-defined standards for enterprise AI" (12 Aug 2026) — https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/08/12/ai-trust-and-security-consortium-launches-to-set-peer-defined-standards-for-enterprise-ai/

Claims sourced here (as reported): The EU AI Act's transparency duties have been enforceable since 2 August 2026 — including obligations to disclose that a user is interacting with AI and to machine-readable-label AI-generated or manipulated content — with penalties up to the higher of €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover. These duties are directly relevant to ChatGPT ads launching in the EEA, where paid content must be disclosed and labeled. The AI Trust and Security Consortium (AITSC) launched on 12 August 2026 with a founding cohort of roughly 50 enterprise security and technology leaders.

Claims sourced here: The MCP 2026-07-28 specification introduced a stateless protocol core (removing the `initialize`/`initialized` handshake and `Mcp-Session-Id`), cacheable list results, hardened authorization, and a stabilized Enterprise-Managed Authorization extension.
