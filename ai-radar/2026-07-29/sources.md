- The Local News, "Why AI Agents Fail in Production: Governance, Orchestration, and Reliability" (7 Jul 2026) — https://thelocalnews.news/2026/07/07/why-ai-agents-fail-in-production-governance-orchestration-and-reliability/

- MarketScale, "Enterprise AI's center of gravity shifts from models to orchestration, governance, and ROI clarity" — https://www.marketscale.com/industries/software-and-technology/enterprise-ais-center-of-gravity-shifts-from-models-to-orchestration-governance-and-roi-clarity

Claims sourced here (as reported): Coverage of the production gap converges on the characterization that "the failure is rarely the model — it is almost always the layer around the model," that the gap between the ~12% of pilots that reach production and the ~88% that do not is "not a technology gap [but] a process design gap, a governance sequencing gap, and an evaluation infrastructure gap," and that "layering an agent onto a broken process produces a faster broken process." As of early 2026, coverage put agent-pilot-to-production rates at roughly 11–14%. These lines are relayed from analyst/press commentary and are not attributed to a single named author.

- John Sviokla, "EP 629 | Daily AI News | July 28, 2026: How to Scale an Agentic Organization: Lessons from DoorDash" — https://johnsviokla.substack.com/p/ep-629-daily-ai-news-july-28-2026

Claims sourced here (as reported): DoorDash published a blueprint for scaling an agentic organization — platform architecture, reusable components, evaluation systems and governance mechanisms. Its AI-agent evaluation harness expanded the quality signal from an average of ~1 employee-submitted feedback to 2,000 auto-graded sessions per day and reduced pre-ship feature validation from hours to minutes; centralized data-governance discipline (defining data-quality principles and compute authorization once) is described as non-negotiable when agentic workflows are in play, since "exposing the wrong table to an agent" can cause "a hallucinated result [to arrive] with full confidence."
