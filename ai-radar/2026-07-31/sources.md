## The story — enterprise AI failure moves from the answer to the last mile

### ChatSee research: failures shifting beyond hallucinations (29 July 2026)

- Morningstar / PR Newswire, "New Research Finds Enterprise AI Failures Are Shifting Beyond Hallucinations as Companies Move from Chatbots to Agents" (29 Jul 2026) — https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20260729sf14924/new-research-finds-enterprise-ai-failures-are-shifting-beyond-hallucinations-as-companies-move-from-chatbots-to-agents
- itbrief, "ChatSee warns of AI failures beyond hallucinations" — https://itbrief.news/story/chatsee-warns-of-ai-failures-beyond-hallucinations
- citybiz, "ChatSee.ai Raises $6.5M as Enterprises Seek to Reduce AI Agent Failures" — https://www.citybiz.co/article/859908/chatsee-ai-raises-6-5m-as-enterprises-seek-to-reduce-ai-agent-failures/
- PR Newswire, "ChatSee.ai Raises $6.5M led by True Ventures to Tackle the Growing Problem of AI Agent Failures" — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/chatseeai-raises-6-5m-led-by-true-ventures-to-tackle-the-growing-problem-of-ai-agent-failures-302798743.html
- SiliconANGLE, "ChatSee raises $6.5M to build 'failure memory' for enterprise AI agents" — https://siliconangle.com/2026/06/12/chatsee-raises-6-5m-build-failure-memory-enterprise-ai-agents/

Claims sourced here (as reported): ChatSee's study of more than 10,000 enterprise AI failure events found that fewer than 10% of failures were hallucination-related; that the largest failure family — resolution and escalation breakdowns — represented 31.1% of observed failures; and that execution and action-related failures increased 62% relative to the Q2 2024 baseline. The research is framed as showing that as enterprises move from AI systems that answer questions to AI systems that perform work, the dominant risk shifts from incorrect content to failed task completion. ChatSee CEO and co-founder Sekhar Sarukkai is quoted: "The enterprise AI question is no longer only whether a model can answer correctly, but whether the AI system can retrieve the right context, take the right action, escalate at the right time, and bring work to resolution," and, on the banking example: "A banking customer can report suspicious activity, receive a polite and compliant response, and still never be escalated for human review. That is a serious enterprise failure even if the model never hallucinated." ChatSee raised $6.5M led by True Ventures.

## The production gap — the last mile at scale

- Morningstar / PR Newswire (above) and general July 2026 coverage relay the standing production-gap figures.
- MIT NANDA research (relayed via coverage) — 95% of generative-AI pilots fail to deliver measurable business impact.

Claims sourced here (as reported): MIT (NANDA) research that 95% of generative-AI pilots deliver no measurable business impact; IDC's finding that 88% of AI-agent proofs-of-concept never reach broad production (for every 33 pilots launched, only four enter live operation); Gartner's findings that more than 40% of agentic-AI projects will be cancelled by the end of 2027 and that 40% of enterprise applications will feature embedded AI agents by year-end (up from less than 5% in 2025).

Claims sourced here: On 2 August 2026, Article 50 transparency obligations become enforceable across all 27 member states (disclosure that a person is interacting with an AI; machine-readable marking of AI-generated content; deepfake labeling) and the Commission's GPAI enforcement powers switch on (information requests, model access, recall/fine powers), both carrying the higher of €15 million or 3% of global annual turnover under Article 99. The high-risk (Annex III) standalone-system obligations were deferred from 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027 under the Digital Omnibus simplification package (Council final approval 29 June 2026).
