## The story — the model is rented, the knowledge is owned, the agent is named, the language is open — and now the question is *who conducts the named, speaking agents into one piece of work* — orchestration is the control point

### The gap — orchestration is the layer almost no one has built (the datable hook)

- Yahoo Finance (mirror) — https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/ai-agents-only-beginning-deloitte-120100295.html

Claims sourced here (as reported): Deloitte's survey "AI agents are only the beginning: the path to agentic transformation" was fielded to 501 senior-manager- to C-suite-level respondents based in the United States across five industries between April and June 2026 (all participating organizations were at least piloting agentic AI). It found that only 15% of organizations have reached scaled, orchestrated, cross-functional multi-agent adoption, and that where scaling is happening it is concentrated in lower-risk, repeatable use cases with near-term ROI — customer service, IT, and engineering — rather than in the high-value core. It also found that 74% of leaders expect nearly half of their business processes to be redesigned or rebuilt around AI agents within four years. Laura Shact, Deloitte's U.S. Technology, Media & Telecommunications (TMT) AI growth leader, is quoted that limited, layered-on approaches "may create the sense of getting ahead with quick wins" but that "thriving in the agentic future will require more" — true transformation requiring investments in work and organization design, intentional leadership and workforce enablement. These are the "orchestration gap" facts: after every agent is named and can speak a common tongue, almost no one has yet built the layer that coordinates them into one outcome.

### The reckoning — un-orchestrated agent sprawl is what gets canceled

- Gartner, "Gartner Predicts Over 40% of Agentic AI Projects Will Be Canceled by End of 2027" (press release, 2025-06-25) — https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027
- MarTech, "Gartner: 40% of agentic AI projects will fail, making humans indispensable" — https://martech.org/gartner-40-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-fail-making-humans-indispensable/
- Forbes (Robert Szczerba), "Why 40% Of Agentic AI Projects May Be Canceled By 2027" — https://www.forbes.com/sites/robertszczerba/2026/07/07/why-40-of-agentic-ai-projects-may-be-canceled-by-2027/
- ecorpIT, "40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by 2027" — https://ecorpit.com/agentic-ai-project-cancellation-governance-cost-controls-2026/
- THE D*AI*LY BRIEF, "89% of AI Agent Pilots Never Scale: Gartner's 2026 Data" — https://www.beri.net/article/ai-agent-adoption-enterprise-2026-gartner-idc

Claims sourced here (as reported): Gartner projects that more than 40% of agentic-AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027, driven by escalating costs, unclear business value, and inadequate risk controls; a separate Gartner forecast expects roughly 40% of enterprises to demote or decommission autonomous agents by 2027 after governance failures surface in production. Coverage also relays that Gartner logged a 1,445% rise in multi-agent-system inquiries from Q1 2024 to Q2 2025; that a large majority of AI-agent pilots (cited around 89%) fail to reach production, with a minority delivering strong ROI; and that only about one in five organizations has a mature governance model for autonomous agents. A frequently cited cost figure holds that an orchestrated agent interaction can run near US$1.20 versus roughly US$0.04 for a 2023 chatbot call (~30×). These are the "cost of the roar" figures: coordination, not appetite, is the bottleneck, and the un-orchestrated projects are the ones getting cut. (Cost and pilot-scaling percentages rest on secondary/trade summaries and are flagged accordingly.)

### The prize — orchestration is where the exponential value lives

- Deloitte Insights, 2026 TMT Predictions, "Unlocking exponential value with AI agent orchestration" — https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/technology/technology-media-and-telecom-predictions/2026/ai-agent-orchestration.html
- Deloitte, 2026 TMT Predictions (overview) — https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/technology/technology-media-and-telecom-predictions.html
- BusinessWorld, "Deloitte 2026 TMT Predictions: Narrowing the gap between the promise of AI and its reality" — https://bworldonline.com/spotlight/2026/05/26/752161/deloitte-2026-tmt-predictions-narrowing-the-gap-between-the-promise-of-ai-and-its-reality/

Claims sourced here (as reported): Deloitte's 2026 TMT Predictions estimate the global agentic-AI market at roughly US$8.5 billion in 2026, potentially reaching about US$35 billion by 2030 — and up to ~30% higher (on the order of US$45 billion by 2030) for enterprises that orchestrate agents more thoughtfully by addressing the associated challenges and risks. Deloitte frames the thesis that autonomous AI agents may be transformational but that orchestration is the key to intelligent automation and to unlocking the exponential value of agentic systems. Cited as the "prize": the multiplier is orchestration, not another model or more agents.

- Linux Foundation / Agentic AI Foundation (A2A joining the AAIF alongside Anthropic's MCP under vendor-neutral governance) — https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/a2a-protocol-surpasses-150-organizations-lands-in-major-cloud-platforms-and-sees-enterprise-production-use-in-first-year

Carried here as the "score": the open, shared standards settled in this week's prior edition ("The Lingua Franca," 27 Aug) are the notation orchestration is built on top of; interoperability is the precondition, orchestration is the layer above it.
